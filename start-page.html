The Workflow

How it works

From your raw initial concept to launch-day celebrations — here is the streamlined path.

01

Plan & Set Up

Verify your eligibility, prepare your ID/bank details, and select a primary category and subcategory.

02

Build Your Page

Clearly explain your project, upload a video prototype, set tiered reward levels, and define your funding goal.

03

Review & Submit

Submit your campaign for human review (1–3 days) and link your Stripe account to verify identity.

04

Launch & Promote

Share your preview link, click 'I'm ready to launch' once approved, and continually engage your backers.

Common questions

Is there a fee to list?

No upfront fees. We take a small platform fee only when your campaign is successfully funded.

What kinds of projects do you accept?

We're category-agnostic — technology, art, music, community, food, fashion and more. What matters is authenticity and impact.

How long does the review take?

We review every submission within 48 hours and provide actionable feedback regardless of the outcome.

Do I keep the money if I don't hit my goal?

Yes — ideakicks uses a flexible funding model. You keep what you raise, with no penalties.
